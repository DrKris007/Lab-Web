About Us

City Public Library was established to provide free and equal access to knowledge for everyone in our community. Since our founding, we have grown to house thousands of books, journals, and digital materials covering a wide range of subjects.

Our mission is to promote literacy, lifelong learning, and a love for reading. We offer a quiet study environment, a children's reading corner, and free internet access for all visitors.

Opening Hours: Monday - Saturday, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
